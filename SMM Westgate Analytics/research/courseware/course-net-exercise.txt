Progress Reports

At the end of Chapter 7, we discussed the features of your Website Analytics from The Social Media Magnet Dashboard.  We descriptively went through each analytic that is collected by our Magnet Analytics software and demonstrated how these metrics were assessed (graded) and how those assessments (grades) were arranged into categorical elements (Brand Awareness, Brand Acquisition, and Brand Retention) in order to help you assess your own campaign. Again, this assessment of your own campaign occurs as you examine your grades across each category or individual metric (users, new users, page views, etc.).  Remember, that your individual grades across each metric or category are benchmarked against the mean and standard deviation of your class.  Therefore, if you have a grade reported as 80, then you are right on the mean.  A 70 is one standard deviation below the mean, while 90 is one standard deviation above the mean.  Hence, if you are sitting at an 80, you know you have room to grow your success in this benchmark.  The purpose of this progress report is to evaluate your strengths and weaknesses using your Magnet Analytics grades as the benchmark.  Then you should examine other students’ projects to see how you might be able to identify opportunities where you can improve your weaknesses, but also how you might defend your strengths from the threat of other students improving their weaknesses.

Therefore, for this progress report, one of three that you will complete during your practicum, we want you to conduct a SWOT Analysis for this self-assessment.  That is, we want you to describe the internal Strengths and Weaknesses that your campaign currently exhibits using your grading metrics reported within the Magnet Analytics.  Then we want you to discuss the external Opportunities and Threats that you perceive to exist by conducting some external analysis of other students in your class.  Your professor has a list of all of the websites for the students in your class.  If he or she has not provided that list to you, then ask for it to be posted via email or Blackboard/Moodle/Canvas, so that you can start to assess other students’ campaigns against yours.  For this first report, you may start with your own group members, and then branch out to other students for the second and third progress reports.  Conducting these SWOT analyses will prepare you for your Final Report, which will also include a final SWOT.

FOR COURSE CREDIT:  This progress report should be 1-2 pages, single-spaced, size 12 Times New Roman, Arial, Calibri, or Cambria font with 1-inch margins.  Do NOT double space, and do not type less than one page.  These progress reports are all worth more than double other assignments because of their importance.  Please be sure to meet your length requirement for each memo.  Also, please check your syllabus for the assignment due dates (three different dates throughout the semester), as you will hand these in to your professor on those assigned days.
