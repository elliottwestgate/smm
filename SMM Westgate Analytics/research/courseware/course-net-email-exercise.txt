Email Analysis Assignment

Using the reporting functions of MailChimp, give an overview of the entire email campaign.  What has worked for you?  What hasn’t worked for you?  Discuss your best email to date and why you believe it was successful.  Discuss your worst email to date and why you think it wasn’t successful.  In this particular discussion, highlight any seasonality or anomalies that may have been present.  Finally, in the last paragraph, discuss a potential A/B test that you could run to test something about your email strategy.  What would you run and why?
