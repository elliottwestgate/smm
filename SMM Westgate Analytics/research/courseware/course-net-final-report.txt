Social Media Final Report Instructions – PowerPoint Report

Directions:  Your final project is to create a report presentation in PowerPoint regarding your social media campaign. The goal of this final report is to teach your professor what you have learned about social media and provide some insight into real takeaways for your classroom experience.

First, please reflect on why you signed up for this course and what you expected to learn.  Then provide your opinion on whether you met that goal describing why or why not you think this objective was met.  In other words, it is ok to be a little critical of any certain area of the class that you thought would be different than expected.

Next, you are to perform a SWOT analysis of your social media campaign.  What internal strengths and weaknesses did you possess and why?  What external opportunities did you exploit and what external threats could you not overcome?  Here, please use your classmates as the competition for the external analysis, not other bloggers like before.  In other words, what did they do that you could NOT duplicate or measure up to when it came to your own campaign.  This will require you to do some external research on their blogs and social media pages, as you compare your final product to their final product.  Your professor will provide access to this data so that you can create your comparison pages.

Finally, you need to explain why you believe your campaign was successful.  What drove success for you?  Which social media channels were best, and why?  What type of blog post worked and why?  What were your Google and MailChimp stats?  Did you set goals for yourself, and did you meet those goals?  What is the biggest takeaway from the class?  Remember, that you need to include all of your social media analytics here to be able to measure success.  Please include your Social Media Magnet Comparisons (You vs. Your Class Average) and also a visual depiction of your Brand Awareness, Brand Acquisition, and your Brand Retention metrics. (NOTE: You can access these three specifically from your professor who can export an excel file from his/her dashboard from the last week of the practicum).  Don’t forget this part, it is very important! 

As to the format, it is completely open to your own design.  We don’t want to limit your possibilities, so make it professional and unique to you.  This project is due BY EMAIL before class on the due date listed in your syllabus.  In order for your professor to grade these in an appropriate amount of time before the end of the semester, you absolutely must turn these into your professor on time.  If not, there is a substantial penalty for tardiness, that is, 10 points for each HOUR it is late.

Finally, a PDF is included below as a “Final Report Example.” Please use this as a starting point, NOT a template.  Please make this presentation unique to your campaign and to you.  DON’T COPY THIS SAMPLE REPORT. However, note, your submission should NOT be in PDF format but should be in PowerPoint format so that your professor can read the NOTES section of your PPT file.  (If you prefer to create this report in Keynote or some other presentation software, then you MUST ask your professor for permission.  However, we have found that Keynote is better for actual presentations due to its slide transition ability.  If you are not presenting this report audibly, then we suggest using PowerPoint instead of Keynote).

Again, remember, the notes are critical to the content portion of your grade as you need to include your explanations for each slide in the notes section of the slide (like you would be presenting it in class).  While the slides should include these topics, the explanation of each topic should be included in the notes section so that your professor can grade your content completely!  You will be graded on 50% style of the presentation (i.e. what each slide looks like) and 50% content (i.e. how you explain yourself within the notes sections).
