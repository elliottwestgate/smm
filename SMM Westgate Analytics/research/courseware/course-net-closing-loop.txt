Closing-the-Loop Assignment


This assignment is an extension of the Campaign Congruency In-Class Exercise.  Once you have gathered all of the group member evaluation forms from your group members, who have each independently evaluated your campaign, you are to complete the Closing the Loop memo.  In this memo, you are to write on your findings related to this project.  Therefore, write the memo in this format:

Paragraph #1:  You need to explain what similarities or differences exist in your hub-and-spoke design, per your group’s feedback.  Be sure to speak to its level of congruent or incongruent messaging.

Paragraph #2:  Explain why you think they would have perceived your messaging in this way.  Remember, perception is reality.  If you see consensus in their evaluation, then this is truly your message, whether it was intended or not. Was this the message you intended for them to have?

Paragraph #3:  Explain your primary narrative again.  Remember back to your original narrative exercise, have you strayed off narrative?  If so, why? In other words, explain how your perceived message is different than your intended message.  If you haven’t strayed off the narrative, and it is the same, then explain how you have made a concerted effort to stay on narrative.  Has this been easy or difficult?

Paragraph #4:  What top three recommendations can you provide me for getting back on narrative, or at least, tightening up your narrative.  Remember that I stressed the importance of your narrative at the beginning of the semester.  I said the narrative exercise would be the most important assignment of the semester.  Brands that can’t stay on narrative don’t succeed.  These recommendations to me and yourself are critical for the final push on your project.  Think long and hard about the three recommendations and then outline your implementation of them in this final paragraph.  So again, tell me WHAT they are and HOW you will tactically implement these three strategies about narrative moving forward.

Paragraph #5:  Where there any group member’s evaluations that you did not receive in order to complete your assignment.  This is a mini-peer evaluation for this project.  If there was a group member who didn’t hold up their end of this assignment, please let me know.

DIRECTIONS FOR COURSE CREDIT:  This is your final memo, and is VERY important; therefore, it should be no less than TWO-typed pages in length.  It should be single-spaced, size 12 Times New Roman, Arial, Calibri, or Cambria font with 1-inch margins.  Do NOT double space!  Please check your syllabus for the assignment due date, as you will hand this in to your professor on the day it is due.
