# Image Prompt and Asset Log

## Generation Method

Ten campaign images were created with OpenAI’s built-in image-generation tool. Two publishing assets use existing Aladdin website photography to avoid depicting fictitious employees or a fabricated before-and-after result.

All generated images used this shared production direction:

> Use case: ads-marketing. Create an original, realistic editorial photograph visually consistent with Aladdin Cleaning & Restoration’s current website and In the Loom blog. Use warm natural light, comfortable San Antonio-area homes or authentic service environments, polished but approachable commercial photography, warm neutral interiors, and restrained black, white and deep-red accents. Professionals may wear clean deep-red polos and khaki work pants, but must have no visible branding. Keep the image text-free. No logo, watermark, discount cues, exaggerated cleaning results, luxury-showroom styling, distorted tools or implausible service procedures.

The website images reviewed as visual references were warm, text-free lifestyle and service photographs with wide WordPress featured-image crops. The Aladdin identity uses black, white and red.

## Image Integrity Rules

- Do not name or tag people shown in generated images.
- Do not connect generated people to reviews or testimonials.
- Use real Aladdin employees and real customer-job photography whenever the post claims employee identity, customer proof or a specific outcome.
- Add text, polls and question stickers natively in the publishing platform or in an approved design tool; the supplied images intentionally contain no generated typography.
