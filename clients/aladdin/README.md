# Aladdin Organic Social Media Magnet Campaign

This section contains the strategy sources, editable campaign calendar, first two weeks of publishing copy, and approved visual assets for Aladdin Cleaning & Restoration's organic campaign.

## Contents

- `strategy/`
  - Aladdin Strategy Report, Insights and Actions (the campaign Strategy Canvas source)
  - Aladdin Brand Tone of Voice
- `calendar/`
  - Original 45-Day Rolling Calendar Template
  - Completed, editable 45-day Organic Social Media Magnet campaign workbook
- `content/weeks-01-02/`
  - Website and social copy in editable DOCX and Markdown formats
  - Image manifest and production log
  - Composite image preview
  - Twelve approved, publishing-ready JPG assets

## Campaign timing

- Full rolling calendar: August 31-October 14, 2026
- Completed content package: August 31-September 13, 2026

## Publishing notes

- Use the files in `content/weeks-01-02/images/` as the approved campaign image set.
- The image manifest maps every image to its post or website article.
- Generated people are illustrative and must not be identified as Aladdin employees or customers or paired with testimonial claims.
- The production log identifies posts that use Aladdin-owned website photography.
- Confirm final links, service availability, contact details, and scheduled dates before publication.
