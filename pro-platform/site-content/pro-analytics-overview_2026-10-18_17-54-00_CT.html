The Social Media Magnet model

Connect the hub
and the spokes.

Your website is the Brand hub. Social channels and email help people discover it, return to it, and act. Pro Analytics brings those signals together while preserving the difference between platform activity and website results.

Brand story, promise, belief, and trust provide the framework for interpreting messaging. Your analytics show the movement; your next actions should answer it.

The report benchmarks your business against its own prior period or previous year. It does not infer competitor performance or prove why a result changed.

From results to direction

Your Pro Analytics Review.
Evidence, interpretation, action.

A business-focused report evaluates the selected performance snapshot and available website and social evidence through the Social Media Magnet framework.

  • Executive assessment and visual Message Magnet diagram
  • Traffic, audience balance, goal events, and conversion efficiency
  • Channel, content, email, and social performance where connected
  • Ranked recommendations: what to do, where to do it, and how to measure it
  • A saved report your team can reopen and print

Request it when you need it.

Changing dates and exploring the dashboard do not consume a review. Owners and analysts can request a new Pro Analytics Review; every company member can read saved reviews.

Recommendations focus on messaging and performance. Publishing schedules, campaign execution, and budget changes remain separate decisions. Deeper strategy and organizational assessment can be addressed through Strategy Canvas and Brand Dyno when the evidence calls for them.
