# Awareness, Acquisition and Retention release

Live on socialmediamagnetpro.com, asset version 0.6.23-preview. No .net changes.

## Report

Added a three-stage evidence model before the existing detailed Report. Each stage uses current and frozen previous figures, with within-measure changes. Social visibility and followers remain platform-specific. Organic Social sessions use the GA4 channel, excluding Paid Social. Goal-event frequency remains form plus phone events per 100 sessions, not a unique-user conversion rate. Returning-user balance is distinct from returning-user count movement. Email campaign clicks are distinct from GA4 Email-attributed sessions. Net-follower differences are absolute; rates use percentage-point changes.

The handoff panel connects Awareness to Acquisition, Acquisition to recorded goals, and Retention to return visits. It does not manufacture platform-to-website conversion rates or attribute goals to posts. Zero recorded goal events explicitly prompt tracking verification. Missing provider evidence remains visible.

Social results now collect automatically for the selected dates. Generation counters discard stale responses; the Report still buffers website and email before publishing. Print and new Review requests wait for social collection to reach a complete or explicitly unavailable state.

## Review

The same evidence model reads each saved Review's original snapshot and original channels. It renders compact supporting figures under Awareness, Acquisition and Retention, with interpretation, Move to Make and measurement check. New AI generation requests include validated three-stage assessments and instructions to align the Message Matrix response to the supported imbalance. The seven SMM foundation sections remain at the end. Existing narrative text is not regenerated. No live AI generation or Review allowance was consumed for this release.
