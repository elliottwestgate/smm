Two Frameworks. Distinct Jobs.

Brand Axis

Read the balance of Awareness, Acquisition and Retention around the Brand. The Brand Axis helps assess that balance alongside Loyalty and Engagement, in environmental and competitive contexts.

Pro Analytics brings visibility, hub visits, goal events and return signals into the Report. The diagram uses session change and the returning-to-new-user ratio as directional indicators, not direct measurements of Loyalty, Engagement or competitive pressure.

Message Matrix

Your campaign planning framework: target audiences × content buckets × channels. Align each audience, content bucket and channel handoff with Awareness, Acquisition and Retention, then put the dimensional sequence into the publishing calendar.

Use content signals to choose what to Develop, Reframe or Create. Give each Move an audience, topic, channel, hub destination, owner, publishing date and success measure.

The Brand Axis informs the assessment of the loop. The Message Matrix must align the response or the campaign risks working against itself. Both return to the Brand Story, Promise, Belief and Trust.

Set Up the Right Company

  1. Confirm the company. Use the company switcher when working across clients. Check the name before connecting an account or reading results.
  2. Account → Company: save the website, logo, social profile links and intended service area. Profile links do not authorize reporting; saved locations do not currently filter the Report.
  3. Account → Connections: the Owner connects the correct GA4 property, selects Mailchimp or Constant Contact when used, and authorizes the intended Facebook Page and linked professional Instagram account when available. Check the displayed provider identity and status after returning.
  4. Verify website tracking. Your website/analytics administrator must separately confirm that GA4 and the configured form and phone events are collecting correctly. Connecting Pro Analytics does not install tracking.
  5. Invite the team. Owners manage company connections and members. Owners and Analysts can request new Reviews when enabled; Viewers can read shared reporting. Analytics-only invitations do not enroll someone in Live Campaign courseware.

Shared application credentials are managed by the platform administrator. Customers do not create a developer application or install the old SMM weekly reporting plugin. Complete only the provider-specific fields shown for your company; keep credentials out of email and screenshots.

Read the Report

Start with Awareness → Acquisition → Retention. Read the current and previous results beside each stage, then inspect Where the loop connects. Platform views, website sessions and email clicks remain distinct measures. Use Basis and gaps to understand what is measured and what still needs tracking.

  1. Choose 30, 60 or 90 days, one year, or a custom range.
  2. Set Compare with to None, Prior period or Previous year.
  3. Select Apply dates. Wait for the progress message to clear and confirm the completed dates and timezone before interpreting or printing.
  4. Read traffic, acquisition/retention balance and goal events together. Inspect Sources, Audience, Timeline and the available email/social sections.
  5. Use Print / Save Report for a completed snapshot. Keep the company, report type and timestamp in its filename.
Metric definitions and limits
  • Goal events per 100 sessions: configured form events plus phone events, divided by sessions, multiplied by 100. Events can repeat. This is not the percentage of people who became customers, and a phone click is not a verified call.
  • Returning users: measured website visitors, not confirmed repeat customers. New and returning counts can overlap.
  • Direct: no clear referral attribution. Do not count all Direct traffic as Email.
  • Social posts: views show visibility. Top-post counts can be lifetime views for posts published in the selected period. Compare age, format and exposure before judging content.
  • Email: selected campaigns are grouped by send date; their responses may be lifetime-to-date. Use provider definitions and comparable response time. Privacy protections and automated activity can distort opens and clicks.
  • Traffic quality: GA4 excludes known bots but does not supply the excluded volume. Additional bot screening and service-area filtering are not active. Missing data is not zero; demographic data may be incomplete or Unknown.
  • Deeper website analysis: use GA4 for top-page/blog rankings, engagement-time metrics and separately configured funnel explorations. These are not current native Pro Analytics Report features.

Use a Pro Analytics Review

The Report supplies results. A Pro Analytics Review organizes selected evidence into content direction and Moves to Make. Owners and Analysts request a new Review deliberately, using a valid comparison when directional analysis is needed. Changing dates does not generate one.

If available, upload a Strategy Canvas or Brand Dyno report under Account → Brand foundations before requesting a new Review. Saved Reviews retain the evidence and document versions used at creation; a new upload does not rewrite them.

  1. Brand Axis signals: read the direction and its limitations before deciding what caused it.
  2. Content signals: inspect actual posts, emails and hub content. A ranking is a clue, not a verdict.
  3. Message Matrix: Develop what connects, Reframe a weak handoff or Create a missing dimension across the channels.
  4. Moves to Make: assign the sequence, put it in the publishing calendar and set a follow-up date.

Open the intended saved Review before choosing Print / Save Review. New requested Reviews use the company allowance shown in the workspace. Viewing or printing an existing Review does not use another one. Current Review recommendations focus on organic content; assess paid campaigns separately.
